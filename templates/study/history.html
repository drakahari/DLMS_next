How to read Study history

Reviewed counts complete answers, including incorrect or assisted answers. Finished means Finish Review was saved. A focused session finishes only its selected questions. Newest sessions appear first; answering again in an older session does not move it between pages.

First outcome means the first complete graded answer. Partial selections can expose feedback before that answer. A copied prompt or requested external AI action does not prove an outside answer was read. Legacy responses cannot establish first attempts, assistance or completion.

Missing earlier saves stay flagged. Retry pending saves in their original quiz tab. If no pending saves remain, start a new review; DLMS cannot invent missing responses. Opening this history never repairs, retries or deletes a session. Times use this browser’s timezone when a valid instant is known.
