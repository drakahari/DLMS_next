Receiver requirements and download limits

Study Packs remain a separate format for authored datasets. Each bundle meets the 128 MiB import limit. One download is limited to 1,000 quizzes and 512 MiB; media and question limits may create extra parts. Hotspots are not supported. The bundle keeps distinct labeled choices even when their text matches. Format 2 also preserves original choice order, repeated labels and incomplete content. A receiver with format-2 support is required; older applications reject it. Needs review content is retained, but graded Study and Exam stay unavailable until you explicitly review and edit it. Quizzes and supported media are included; personal results and history are not. Use a full backup for recovery.
