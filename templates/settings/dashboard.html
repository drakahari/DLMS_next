This feature switch controls the trophy case and certification sidebar links, including Training library. Dashboard panels → My Certifications controls only the trophy case; both must be on to show it. Turning this off keeps every record, document, period, allocation and display-count choice. Your separate dashboard-card choice is also preserved. The scoped Restore buttons do not change this switch.

Save applies all visibility choices. Each Restore button immediately restores only its named section to visible, preserving the other section’s saved choices and unrelated settings. Unsaved edits are not applied by Restore. Settings remains in the sidebar even with all optional dashboard content hidden. Use Layout & navigation here to show hidden cards again.
