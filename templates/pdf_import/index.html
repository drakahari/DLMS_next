Current Smart PDF scope

DLMS handles selectable-text PDF question banks and glossary/terminology layouts, screenshot OCR, and optional OCR of explicitly selected low-text question-bank PDF pages. OCR runs locally and never trusts selected or highlighted answers as proof of correctness. It supports 2–26 choices and multiple correct answers, and every OCR draft requires Review & Repair. Temporary source previews are removed after save, cancellation, or expiration. Glossary OCR and arbitrary textbook/chapter interpretation are not enabled.
