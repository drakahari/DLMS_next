Current import scope

DLMS handles selectable-text PDF question banks and glossary/terminology layouts, screenshot OCR, terminology/matching OCR, and optional OCR of explicitly selected low-text question-bank PDF pages. OCR runs locally and never trusts selected or highlighted answers as proof of correctness. Choice OCR supports 2–26 choices and multiple correct answers, and every OCR draft requires Review & Repair. Temporary source files are removed after conversion, save, cancellation, or expiration. Arbitrary textbook/chapter interpretation is not enabled.
