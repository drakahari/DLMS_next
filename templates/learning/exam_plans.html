What these counts mean

Complete incorrect or assisted answers count as reviewed. Finish Review must be acknowledged to finish a full or focused session. Focused sessions never create a whole-source completion. Repeated sessions do not inflate question coverage. First-response mistakes and later corrections are distinct facts from current-version Study sessions; correction does not erase the original difficulty. These factual counts survive a Learning Intelligence reset. Assisted work includes observable answer feedback before a later response.

A Learning Intelligence reset preserves factual history but clears estimates; old facts never recreate cleared mistakes or due dates. A material quiz edit conservatively makes prior whole-quiz coverage historical. Generated copies also require a matching source revision. Legacy evidence cannot prove first attempts or assistance. Older identical questions can share legacy ranking evidence; plan coverage and daily credit still require their actual source identities. Exam evidence informs recommendations but is not a Study completion.
