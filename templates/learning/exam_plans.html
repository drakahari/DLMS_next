Why this?

Each selected question counts once: coverage needs first, then mistakes, due reviews and other practice. “No recorded answer” means no identifiable saved answer, not proof it was never seen. Coverage / fresh evidence includes previously answered material needing current Study coverage, a changed version or post-reset evidence. A mistake that is also due counts under mistakes unless it needs coverage first.

{{ report.stats.today }} included questions already answered today reduce today’s suggested work. The calculated first-pass / fresh-evidence target is {{ report.target }}, within {{ report.remaining_slots }} remaining slots. Each batch contains at most 50 questions.

Coverage needs are spread over {{ report.calendar.days }} study dates before the exam. Remaining slots use existing mistake/due rankings and concept/quiz diversity, then other unique practice. Overlapping reasons count once. Refreshing does not restart the quota. Native review intervals are unchanged.

Today uses {{ plan.config.calendar_timezone }}. Unknown response times cannot earn daily credit. Complete incorrect and assisted answers count as work, not independent success.
