DLMS Medical Terminology — Foundations v1
===========================================

Contents
--------
112 matching pairs:
- Combining forms
- Common medical suffixes
- Common medical prefixes

This file is designed for the DLMS Matching Bank importer. DLMS uses the
term and definition columns; the category column is retained for future
filtering/curation and is safely ignored by the current importer.

Provenance notes
----------------
- NLM's SPECIALIST Lexicon and Lexical Tools are distributed as open resources
  subject to NLM's attribution/redistribution terms.
- NLM documentation describes NC.DB as a database of neoclassical combining
  forms with morpheme, meaning, and type fields.
- The NorQuest College OER was used as a secondary educational cross-check for
  common combining forms, prefixes, and suffixes.
- The short definitions in this CSV were curated/paraphrased for DLMS rather
  than copied as long-form source text.
- Similar or duplicate meanings were deliberately reduced where possible so a
  randomized matching round is less likely to contain ambiguous answer choices.
- This is a study aid, not a clinical reference.
