History & Analytics

Inspect one attempt when you need details; use Analytics for patterns.

Review attempt history

History lists completed attempts by date, score, quiz origin, and mode. Use the origin filters to narrow the full result set, then use Previous and Next to move through history pages. Filters do not delete or change attempts.

Review missed questions

Choose Review for one attempt to open its saved missed-question detail. This is the place to inspect selected answers, correct answers, matching context, or hotspot context, then follow up with Anki or more practice.

Use Analytics for trends

Analytics summarizes attempts by quiz, including average, best result, pass rate, trend, and latest attempt. Use it to identify a quiz that needs attention, then open its latest review or return to focused practice.

Move from results to targeted review

History and Analytics answer “what happened?” Learning Intelligence answers “what topic should I practice next?” when questions have concepts. Use Smart Review, Review Schedule, and Diagnostics from the dedicated guide.
