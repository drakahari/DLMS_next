Study Areas & Case Review

Choose a subject area, or use the dedicated Law case-review workspace.

IT Study

IT Study groups installed IT/Cybersecurity Study Packs. Use its landing page to choose matching, diagrams/images, or the AI Study Pack Builder.

Medical Study

Medical Study is available even before medical content is installed. Add a Medical-domain Study Pack when you want terminology, matching, anatomy, or image practice.

Create and study a Law Case Review

Open Law Study → Create Case Review, enter the case and course, choose the study sections, and generate the AI prompt.
Use the prompt with your AI provider, then return to Import Case Packet and paste the complete packet.
Select Save & Preview Case Packet. DLMS saves the raw packet first, then opens the parser preview.
Review the recognized sections. If they are correct, select Create Case Review From Import.
DLMS opens the exact saved Case Review. Use the case brief, Socratic work, IRAC drill, flashcards, notes, export, and Law Study Anki tools from there.
Why packets are saved first: Saved Imports preserve the original source for provenance, recovery, deletion, or reparsing. They are also the manual-import/archive workspace; they are not an extra required detour in the normal guided path.

Other Studies

Other Studies filters installed packs outside the dedicated IT, Law, and Medical areas. It uses the same validated Study Pack and quiz workflows.
