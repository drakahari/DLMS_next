IT, Law & Medical Study

Understand what each specialized study area adds to the shared DLMS framework.

IT Study

IT Study aggregates installed Study Packs whose content domain is IT/Cybersecurity. Its focused navigation includes Concepts & Matching, Diagrams & Images, and an AI Study Pack Builder link preconfigured for IT/Cybersecurity.

Law Study

Law Study is a specialized case-review workflow rather than only a Study Pack browser. It can create/import case packets, maintain saved case reviews, capture notes, Socratic answers and IRAC work, export review text, and integrate with Law Study Anki tools.

Medical Study

Medical Study is a built-in DLMS capability whose content is supplied by installed Medical-domain Study Packs. It exposes Terminology & Matching, Anatomy & Images, and a Medical-prefilled AI Study Pack Builder. If no medical content is installed, the module remains available and explains that content is optional.
