Add or edit an earned credential

  1. Open My Certifications from the dashboard, sidebar or Settings. Select Add certification.
  2. Enter the name, any issuer, originally earned date and current expiration. Choose Does not expire when appropriate; leave unknown dates blank.
  3. Add a Display badge/logo and an Official certificate independently. Both are optional. Select Save record.

Edit certification corrects current details without creating another renewal period. Optional dates, rules and notes are in expandable sections. Your trophy case keeps expired and superseded achievements; their status remains visible. It never adds sample credentials.

View certificate opens the current period’s official document. It never substitutes a badge. If an older image’s purpose is unclear, it is retained and you can explicitly choose to use it as a certificate too. Changing the badge does not replace the official document.

Display options saves 3, 6 or All cards. View all opens the full collection. In Settings → Layout & navigation, Show My Certifications hides or shows both the trophy case and certification sidebar links. Records, files, display count and the independent dashboard-card preference remain unchanged. The toggle always remains in Settings.

Record training once, apply it where appropriate

  1. Open Training log → Log training. Enter the activity, provider, completion date and actual learning hours. Public course URL, topics, private notes and evidence are optional. Any provider is supported; no provider account is needed.
  2. Choose Apply to certification, then the intended current or historical period.
  3. Record a proposed match, rationale and source. Mark it reviewed when you have checked it. Enter Submitted credit and Accepted credit separately, including partial acceptance.

Example: an eight-hour workshop may support two credentials. For one you propose five credits, submit four and record two accepted. For the other you record its own permitted allocation. The library still contains eight actual hours. Advertised course length and AI advice do not establish eligible credit. Editing the activity’s hours never increases accepted credit.

One allocation per activity per period prevents duplicate counting. Edit an existing allocation rather than entering it again. A reporting date normally uses the completion date; only use an exception with a recorded policy rationale. A saved allocation never awards quiz completion or Study evidence.

Understand annual and full-period progress

Custom credentials have the same tools as presets. Under Renewal requirements, enter the total requirement, unit, credential/policy version, route, source and date checked. Leave unknown requirements blank; DLMS labels them unverified, not zero or unsupported.

Record the actual credit reporting dates and year definition. Calendar years and a recorded anniversary are supported; do not assume either without checking. Irregular reporting years need manual notes and verification. A February 29 anniversary uses February 28 in non-leap years; choose it only if that matches your issuer’s rule.

Distinguish a Mandatory annual minimum from Optional yearly pacing. Annual rows partition the same allocations used in the cycle total. They are not additional credit. Category caps reduce the displayed counted total without changing saved accepted amounts. Category minimums and outstanding conditions remain visible even when the total reaches its target.

Presets are editable starting points, not issuer approval. CISM and CISSP have different annual rules. LPIC exam renewal and optional LPI membership are separate routes; the membership preset leaves the conflicting annual wording for verification. PeopleCert depends on the exact version and route. No personal dates, fees or automatic renewals are inferred. A full bar never proves that submission, membership, fees, audit or other conditions are satisfied.

Record an actual renewal

Select Record renewal for a new renewal, not to correct current details. Check the previous expiration, enter the renewed expiration and optionally the recording date and replacement certificate. Early renewals are supported: the recording date can precede the old expiration and new effective start. Leave unknown effective dates blank.

The new period becomes current. Earlier periods, documents and allocations remain in history, with no credit carried forward. Without a replacement document, the new period shows Add certificate; the old certificate stays labeled in history. Use Correct historical details for a historical correction. Conflicting known dates are rejected with a specific explanation. Period order does not silently change when dates are corrected.

Renewal relationships records an explicit direction and an earning or renewing trigger between any two saved credentials. Keep the supporting rule and conditions. Nothing changes automatically. After issuer confirmation, choose Record confirmed target renewal, verify the new expiration and confirm that one event. Reverse and transitive relationships are never assumed. No CompTIA mappings are enabled automatically.

Find renewal matches without sending your evidence

  1. Select Find renewal matches. Select any saved credentials and training to include. Expiring, non-superseded credentials are preselected; you control the selection.
  2. Enter a question and select Prepare prompt. Review and edit the exact text.
  3. Select Copy prompt, then Open preferred AI and paste it yourself. If copying is unavailable, use your device’s Copy action on the selected text.

The text includes selected public course details, actual hours and relevant credential/rule fields. Private notes, credential IDs, attachments and extracted contents are excluded. Anything you manually add is your choice. Opening the provider sends no prompt; there are no background AI calls. AI suggestions never change credit, dates, renewal or Study records.

Check suggestions against current official rules. Record useful matches through Training log → Apply to certification, with rationale, source and proposed credit. Reviewing, submitting and recording acceptance are separate manual actions.

Settings → AI Integration has an editable portfolio prompt and scoped default reset. Keep {{portfolio_context}} for the selected information. Save AI settings to persist changes. The existing single-certification Ask AI about renewal prompt and Study prompts remain independent.

Evidence, backup and safe recovery

Badge images support PNG, JPEG and WebP. Certificates also support PDFs. Raster images are stored as lossless PNGs with orientation applied and metadata removed; keep original files separately if needed. Each original and stored file is limited to 10 MiB; images to 16 million pixels; PDFs to 1,000 pages. Encrypted PDFs, executable images, scripts and embedded PDF files are rejected. PDFs download for your own viewer. Managed certification evidence has a 256 MiB total limit.

Backup & Restore includes certification records and uploaded evidence. Older backups remain supported; restoring one without certifications returns the certification collection to that backup’s empty state. Restore first creates the existing safety backup. A newer upgraded database cannot be opened by an older build.

Before upgrading, keep a verified pre-upgrade backup. To roll back: stop DLMS normally, preserve the upgraded data root separately, and restore the pre-upgrade backup with the matching older build in a separate supported data root. Verify its data before making it your working profile. Switching binaries alone does not roll back a database. Restoring an older backup loses subsequent changes throughout the profile, including Study history and settings. Keep the upgraded data and a current backup separately. Never edit schema numbers.

A missing attachment does not delete the record. Reattach a verified copy through its edit form. Removal requires confirmation; shared training and evidence still referenced by other records remain. Create a backup before removing records. Learning Intelligence, Study-history, quiz-library and content resets keep certifications; a full-data reset removes them with all profile data.

If saving fails, no partial record is applied. Reselect any upload and retry the same form. If another tab or a restore made the form stale, reopen the record and compare your intended changes before saving. Do not clear browser storage or reset learning as a workaround.

Screenshot maintenance

Refresh these images only from disposable sample records using the certification browser workflow and DLMS_CERTIFICATION_CAPTURE_DIR. Capture the actual form, period and portfolio controls; crop with the existing Help screenshot helper. Keep meaningful alt text, captions and enlargement links synchronized with the UI. Do not photograph personal certificates.
