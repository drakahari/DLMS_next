My Certifications

Keep earned credentials, renewal evidence and training together. Exam preparation stays in Exam Plans.

Add a credential · Training and credit · Renewal cycles · Ask AI safely · Backups and recovery

Add an earned credential

  1. On the dashboard, find My Certifications below Quick tools. It remains available when Quick tools is hidden. You can also open My Certifications from Settings.
  2. Select Add certification. Enter its name, issuer and earned date. Choose Non-expiring credential if appropriate. A badge is optional.
  3. Select Save record. Open Edit cycle and evidence to record an expiration date, renewal due date, requirements, credit unit, official policy link and certificate.

Your records are not issuer verification. No credential artwork, dates or renewal rules are supplied automatically. Dates are calendar dates and stay the same across time zones.

On the dashboard, Display options saves 3, 6 or All cards. View all opens the collection when limited. Layout & navigation can hide or restore the card without deleting records.

Training hours and credit are different

  1. Open Training log → Log training. Record the activity, provider, completion date and hours, with optional notes and evidence.
  2. Open a certification cycle and select Allocate training. Choose the saved activity and record the hours associated with that cycle.
  3. Record Submitted credit and Accepted credit separately, in the cycle’s named unit. Partial acceptance is allowed. DLMS does not convert hours into credit.

Example: you record an eight-hour workshop, associate six hours with a cycle, submit five credits and record three accepted credits. Those totals remain distinct. Editing the workshop’s hours does not increase its accepted credit. One activity has one allocation per cycle; edit that allocation instead of entering it twice. You may explicitly allocate the activity to another certification if the issuer’s policy permits.

Training does not award quiz completion or learning credit. The accepted/required comparison is your recorded progress, not proof of renewal or exam readiness.

Start another renewal cycle

Choose Start new renewal cycle after the prior cycle starts. Enter its dates, requirements and unit. Earlier cycles and their evidence stay accessible in expandable rows; training allocations and credits never carry forward automatically. The latest recorded cycle appears first. A renewal recorded date is your record, not automatic issuer approval. A credit unit cannot be changed once allocations exist; start a new cycle for a different unit.

Ask AI without sending your evidence

  1. Select Ask AI about renewal. Choose a cycle and explicitly select certification information, renewal information and any allocated training to include.
  2. Enter your question, then select Prepare prompt. Review and edit the exact text.
  3. Select Copy prompt, then Open preferred AI and paste it yourself. If copying is unavailable, the text remains selected for manual Copy.

Notes, credential IDs, attachments and extracted file contents are excluded from the prepared context. Anything you manually add to the editable text is your choice. Opening the provider sends no prompt. AI guidance never changes your credits, renewal status or Study evidence.

Settings → AI Integration controls the provider and editable certification default prompt. Restore certification prompt default updates that field; save AI settings to persist it. Keep the {{certification_context}} placeholder. The default asks for current official issuer rules and cited sources, possible eligibility and missing information, distinguishing guidance from issuer approval.

Evidence, backup and safe recovery

Badge images support PNG, JPEG and WebP. Certificates also support PDFs. Raster images are stored as lossless PNGs with orientation applied and metadata removed; keep original files separately if needed. Each original and stored file is limited to 10 MiB; images to 16 million pixels; PDFs to 1,000 pages. Encrypted PDFs, executable images, scripts and embedded PDF files are rejected. PDFs download for your own viewer. Managed certification evidence has a 256 MiB total limit.

Backup & Restore includes certification records and uploaded evidence. Older backups remain supported; restoring one without certifications returns the certification collection to that backup’s empty state. Restore first creates the existing safety backup. A newer upgraded database cannot be opened by an older build.

Before upgrading, keep a verified pre-upgrade backup. To roll back: stop DLMS normally, preserve the upgraded data root separately, and restore the pre-upgrade backup with the matching older build in a separate supported data root. Verify its data before making it your working profile. Switching binaries alone does not roll back a database. Never edit schema numbers.

A missing attachment does not delete the record. Reattach a verified copy through its edit form. Removal requires confirmation; shared training and evidence still referenced by other records remain. Create a backup before removing records. Learning Intelligence, Study-history, quiz-library and content resets keep certifications; a full-data reset removes them with all profile data.

If saving fails, no partial record is applied. Reselect any upload and retry the same form. If another tab or a restore made the form stale, reopen the record and compare your intended changes before saving. Do not clear browser storage or reset learning as a workaround.

Screenshot maintenance
