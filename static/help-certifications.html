My Certifications

Keep earned credentials, renewal evidence and training together. Exam preparation stays in Exam Plans.

Add or edit an earned credential

  1. Open My Certifications from the dashboard, sidebar or Settings. Select Add certification.
  2. Enter the name, any issuer, originally earned date and current expiration. Choose Does not expire when appropriate; leave unknown dates blank.
  3. Add a Display badge/logo and an Official certificate independently. Both are optional. Select Save changes.

Edit certification corrects current details without creating another renewal period. Use Set renewal goal / Edit goal for a target, unit and planning deadline. Manage detailed requirements is a separate optional editor for policy and reporting rules. Saving any one task preserves fields in the others. Your trophy case keeps expired and superseded achievements; their status remains visible. It never adds sample credentials.

View certificate opens the current period’s official document. It never substitutes a badge. If an older image’s purpose is unclear, it is retained and you can explicitly choose to use it as a certificate too. Changing the badge does not replace the official document.

Display options opens Settings, where Number to show accepts All or any positive whole number. Cards keep their readable size and wrap; a count larger than your collection shows all saved credentials. Choose the saved order by name, earned date or expiration. View all opens the full collection. In Settings → Layout & navigation, Show My Certifications — dashboard and sidebar hides or shows both the trophy case and certification sidebar links. Records, files, display count and the independent dashboard-card preference remain unchanged. The toggle always remains in Settings. The separate Dashboard panels → My Certifications — dashboard trophy case only preference hides only that panel. Both must be on to show the trophy case. Other edits need Save layout & navigation; Restore defaults applies immediately and asks before discarding unsaved edits.

Log a course and choose renewals

  1. Open Training library and select Add training. Enter the course title, provider, completion date, Hours and Minutes. Supporting evidence is optional; descriptions, links and private notes are under Details.
  2. Select Save and choose certifications. Check only the credentials this course may support. Nothing is selected automatically.
  3. Review each Estimated contribution, then select Use toward renewal. Current periods are selected initially; Details offers historical periods.

Example: enter 9 Hours and 22 Minutes. DLMS keeps the full 562 minutes. For an hours-based goal, it offers an editable estimate of 9.37 hours. For credits, CPE or another unit, enter your estimate or leave it unknown; learning time is not automatically eligible credit. Choose two credentials if appropriate: the library still contains one course and 9 h 22 min of learning time.

All selected contributions save together. If any selection fails, none are changed; correct the error and retry the same form. Retrying cannot add duplicate contributions. Cancel leaves the saved course in the library without applying it. Already linked rows show the saved period, contribution, associated time and reported credit. Select a row to reveal estimate editing; saving it updates the estimate. Unchecked rows remain unchanged. Removal is a separate confirmed action. A course lasting 9 h 22 min can legitimately retain an older 9 h associated contribution and nine submitted/accepted credits; editing the course never increases those facts.

Optional reporting and older durations

Edit reporting details offers submitted and accepted credit, partial amounts, categories, sources, reporting-date exceptions and private notes under Details. Accepted credit is never filled in automatically. Editing course time does not increase credit or rewrite existing contributions. A legacy duration such as 9.37 decimal hours displays exactly as 9 h 22.2 min; it is not rounded to 22 minutes. Minutes must be below 60; fractional minutes retain older precision.

A reporting date normally uses completion. Only use an exception with a recorded rationale. A course may support multiple credentials, but only one contribution per course and period is saved. No training action awards quiz completion or Study evidence.

Set a goal and understand estimated progress

Open a certification to see estimated progress, remaining amount and deadline. If its goal is unknown, select Set renewal goal to open a short form for target amount, unit and optional planning deadline. The Planning deadline is your personal target. It does not change Expires or the separately recorded Renewal deadline. Clearing it clears only your planning target. An optional annual goal is pacing. If you recorded a mandatory annual minimum, the short form shows and preserves it; correct that rule only in Manage detailed requirements. Changing a unit after recording contributions is blocked to avoid reinterpreting credit. You do not need advanced policy settings to log a course or set a planning goal.

Each course counts once: its estimate, at least its recorded accepted credit, or submitted credit when no estimate exists. These stages are not added together. Estimates need issuer eligibility checks; reaching a goal does not confirm renewal.

You can save an optional annual pacing amount in the short goal form, but dated annual progress needs reporting dates and a year definition in Manage detailed requirements. Until then, use the cycle estimate; DLMS does not assume calendar years. Annual progress appears when those dates are recorded. Mandatory annual minimums remain distinct from optional pacing. Details keeps accepted-credit totals, category caps and other conditions visible when needed. Unknown rules remain editable and unverified.

Three independent dates: Planning deadline is your optional target; Renewal deadline is a due date you record, not issuer verification; Expires is the certificate’s expiration. The summary shows Planning deadline first, otherwise Renewal deadline, otherwise Expires, always with its matching label. None sets the credit reporting period. Dates stay on the same calendar day in every timezone.

An old shared date is preserved as Unclassified, without being used as either deadline. If you know its meaning, open Manage detailed requirements → Classify this date and choose one meaning. Saving never fills both deadlines or overwrites a different date. The original value stays available. Previously overwritten dates cannot be recovered without a backup.

Optional requirements and detailed reporting

Open Manage detailed requirements when you need policy sources, reporting exceptions or categories. Add category adds one row; unused rows are not shown. Annual amount and reporting-year controls appear when relevant. Credential version belongs to the earned credential; policy version identifies the rules you recorded.

Load suggested requirements shows the source and verification status first. Confirm that you want to replace form values, review them, then save. Cancel keeps your custom values. Loading a suggestion never verifies eligibility or changes saved credit.

Record the issuer’s reporting dates and year definition rather than assuming calendar years. Calendar and anniversary years are supported; irregular years need manual verification. February 29 anniversaries use February 28 in non-leap years. Category caps adjust progress without changing saved amounts; contributions outside recorded reporting dates are excluded. Approximate progress includes unclassified courses, while detailed accepted-credit progress requires matching categories when categories are configured.

Presets are editable starting points. No policy research, fees or automatic renewal is inferred. Custom issuers have the same tools. A full bar does not establish that submission, membership, fees, audit or other conditions are satisfied.

Record an actual renewal

Open Manage certification → Record renewal for a new renewal, not to correct current details. Check the previous expiration, enter the renewed expiration and optionally the recording date and replacement certificate. Early renewals are supported: the recording date can precede the old expiration and new effective start. Leave unknown effective dates blank.

The new period becomes current. Earlier periods, documents and allocations remain in history, with no credit carried forward. Without a replacement document, the new period shows Add certificate; the old certificate stays labeled in history. Use Correct historical details for a historical correction. Conflicting known dates are rejected with a specific explanation. Period order does not silently change when dates are corrected.

Renewal relationships records an explicit direction and an earning or renewing trigger between any two saved credentials. Keep the supporting rule and conditions. Nothing changes automatically. After issuer confirmation, choose Record confirmed target renewal, verify the new expiration and confirm that one event. Reverse and transitive relationships are never assumed. No CompTIA mappings are enabled automatically.

Find renewal matches without sending your evidence

  1. Select Find renewal matches. Select any saved credentials and training to include. Renewable, non-superseded credentials with a recorded expiration are preselected. Unknown-status credentials remain available to select yourself; uncheck anything you do not want included.
  2. Enter a question and select Prepare prompt. Open Review / edit prepared text to inspect the exact text. Selection counts show which certifications and courses were included. If you change the selection or question, prepare the prompt again before copying. With no course selected, ask only about requirements; select a course for matching.
  3. Select Copy & open AI, then paste into your chosen provider yourself. The smaller Copy only action remains available. If copying fails, select the preview and use your device’s Copy action. If no provider tab opens, expand Nothing opened, or need help copying? and use Open AI manually. DLMS cannot detect every launch failure. The visible status reports copying separately from opening; ordinary LAN HTTP is supported without weakening browser security.

The text includes selected public course details, actual hours/minutes and relevant credential/rule fields. Private notes, credential IDs, attachments and extracted contents are excluded. Anything you manually add is your choice. Opening the provider sends no prompt; there are no background AI calls. AI suggestions never change credit, dates, renewal or Study records.

Check suggestions against current official rules. Record useful matches through Training library → Use toward renewal, with rationale, source and proposed credit. Reviewing, submitting and recording acceptance are separate manual actions.

Settings → AI Integration has an editable portfolio prompt and scoped default reset. Keep {{portfolio_context}} for the selected information. All prompt editors and the shared provider use one Save AI settings action at the bottom. The sticky action row shows unsaved changes. Defaults edit only their own field; Cancel reloads saved values after confirmation. A failed save retains your edits. The selected provider is shown separately from Copy & open AI and also controls Study question/answer AI review. The existing single-certification Ask AI about renewal prompt and Study prompts remain independent.

Evidence, backup and safe recovery

Badge images support PNG, JPEG and WebP. Certificates also support PDFs. Raster images are stored as lossless PNGs with orientation applied and metadata removed; keep original files separately if needed. Each original and stored file is limited to 10 MiB; images to 16 million pixels; PDFs to 1,000 pages. Encrypted PDFs, executable images, scripts and embedded PDF files are rejected. PDFs download for your own viewer. Managed certification evidence has a 256 MiB total limit.

Backup & Restore includes certification records and uploaded evidence. Older backups remain supported; restoring one without certifications returns the certification collection to that backup’s empty state. Restore first creates the existing safety backup. A newer upgraded database cannot be opened by an older build.

Before upgrading, keep a verified pre-upgrade backup. To roll back: stop DLMS normally, preserve the upgraded data root separately, and restore the pre-upgrade backup with the matching older build in a separate supported data root. Verify its data before making it your working profile. Switching binaries alone does not roll back a database. Restoring an older backup loses subsequent changes throughout the profile, including Study history and settings. Keep the upgraded data and a current backup separately. Never edit schema numbers.

A missing attachment does not delete the record. Reattach a verified copy through its edit form. Removal requires confirmation; shared training and evidence still referenced by other records remain. Create a backup before removing records. Learning Intelligence, Study-history, quiz-library and content resets keep certifications; a full-data reset removes them with all profile data.

If saving fails, no partial record is applied. Reselect any upload and retry the same form. If another tab or a restore made the form stale, reopen the record and compare your intended changes before saving. Do not clear browser storage or reset learning as a workaround.
