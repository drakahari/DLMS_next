Getting Started & Navigation

Find the right workspace, create practice, and decide what to do next.

Find your workspace

Use the sidebar for Dashboard, Quiz Library, Build Quiz, Study Packs, subject study areas, History, Analytics, Learning Intelligence, Anki Tools, Settings, Content Packs, Image Study Editor, and Help. On smaller screens, open the same navigation with the menu button.

Personalize visible study areas

Use Settings → Navigation or the sidebar’s Customize navigation link to hide or show IT Study, Law Study, Medical Study, and Other Studies. This is only a navigation preference: direct links and saved data remain available.

A normal study workflow

Choose a source: Build Quiz, Smart PDF, a Study Pack, or a subject workspace.
Generate or open a quiz and choose Study Mode for feedback or Exam Mode for a completed attempt.
Use History for one attempt, Analytics for patterns, and Learning Intelligence for tagged-topic review.
Export selected material to Anki or printable cards when you want additional review outside a quiz.

Local and trusted-network use

DLMS starts for local use by default. Advanced users can intentionally start it with a non-local --host value for a trusted LAN. DLMS does not provide login-based access control in that mode, so do not expose it directly to the public internet or an untrusted network.
