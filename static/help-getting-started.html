How DLMS is organized

Use the left navigation as the map of the application. Dashboard is the home view, Quiz Library stores generated quizzes, Build Quiz creates new quiz material, and Study Packs exposes installed reusable study content. IT, Law, and Medical provide domain-focused workflows. History and Analytics report results, while System tools manage settings, content, images, help, and maintenance.

Quiz Library

The Quiz Library is where saved/generated quizzes are reopened. A quiz created from pasted text, Smart PDF, a terminology bank, a Study Pack, or another builder becomes a normal DLMS quiz and remains usable independently of the source workflow that produced it.

A typical workflow

Choose a source: build a quiz, import a Smart PDF, or launch content from a Study Pack.
Review or configure the source material before generating a quiz.
Open the resulting quiz from the Quiz Library and choose Exam Mode or Study Mode as appropriate.
Use History and Analytics to review results. Export to Anki when long-term spaced repetition is useful.
Source vs. quiz: reusable source banks and Study Packs are source material. Generated quizzes are copies used for practice. Deleting a source bank does not intentionally remove previously generated quizzes.
