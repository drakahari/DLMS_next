Getting Started & Navigation

Find the right workspace, create practice, and decide what to do next.

Find your workspace

Use the sidebar for Dashboard, Quiz Library, Build Quiz, Study Packs, subject study areas, Exam History, Analytics, Learning Intelligence, Anki Tools, Settings, Content Packs, Image Study Editor, and Help. On smaller screens, open the same navigation with the menu button.

Read your dashboard

Study next highlights your plan or recommended practice. A response waiting to save takes priority: use Resolve Study saves or Finish saving Exam first. Workload and save warnings stay visible even when practice is ready. Open Why this suggestion? for the reason, coverage detail and estimate assumptions. Included questions reviewed describes coverage, not mastery. When other recommendations are available, open More review options for optional review across your Learning Scope. Due-question and concept review take precedence over broader Adaptive Study suggestions; these options may include material outside your plan. A browser-only Resume entry does not remove shared review suggestions. Resume keeps your unfinished batch; starting another review creates a separate quiz.

Continue studying keeps your Last regular quiz separate from generated practice. It appears after a response has been saved to DLMS. An unfinished review offers Resume; a finished review offers Open current quiz. A browser checkpoint can also exist before any response is saved. Two entries combine only when they identify the same unfinished Study session and unchanged questions.

Open Manage browser resume point for the separate Clear browser resume point action. Clearing removes only the named checkpoint in this browser. Saved Study progress can still offer Resume afterward, and another local checkpoint may appear. Answers waiting to save must be resolved first.

After a finished review, Last regular quiz offers Open current quiz; it does not ask you to resume completed work. Open Study details, or Manage browser resume point when a browser checkpoint exists, for saved times and recovery details.

Your activity contains Continue your quiz sequence and Latest Exam completed. The sequence uses your last regular quiz with a saved Study answer. It offers the next regular quiz in the same folder only after a saved Finish Review for unchanged content. While unfinished, use the existing Resume action above. The saved order is the folder’s Quiz Library order: change it with drag-and-drop or Up and Down. Generated focused practice does not replace the anchor, and reviewed quizzes are not skipped. Hidden, excluded or unavailable material stops the sequence with an explanation. At the end, use Choose another quiz; DLMS never jumps folders. Opening a suggested quiz earns no review credit. Latest Exam completed keeps its score and Review attempt link.

Resume entries show their quiz ID, question position and browser-saved time so generated quizzes with the same title remain distinguishable. Missing times or positions are labeled unavailable. Clearing a browser checkpoint and completing a saved Study review remain separate actions.

Use Study History for finished reviews and partial Study sessions. Use Exam History for saved Exam results and clearly labeled legacy Study results. Quick tools keeps your enabled shortcuts together. Results overview summarizes Exam scores and older saved Study results. Finished and partial Study reviews remain in Study History, separately from these scores. Open What is counted? for the distinction.

Personalize visible study areas

Open Settings → Layout & navigation, or use Customize navigation in the sidebar. Settings stays in the sidebar even when every dashboard card is hidden. IT, Law and Medical have independent Show on dashboard and Show in sidebar choices; Other Studies is sidebar only. Select Save layout & navigation to apply your choices. Hidden areas keep their content, progress, history and direct links.

See Layout & navigation Help for Hide from both, dashboard-only cards and the separate Restore controls.

A normal study workflow

What should I study right now? Start with Study next on the Dashboard. It combines the most useful current actions from due questions, topic needs, unfinished work, and existing study activity without creating another scoring system.
Choose a source: Build Quiz, PDF & Image Import, a Study Pack, or a subject workspace.
Generate or open a quiz and choose Study Mode for feedback or Exam Mode for a completed attempt.
Return to Study next for the recommended next action. Use Study History for saved Study sessions or Exam History for one saved result, Analytics for patterns, and Learning Intelligence when you want topic detail or a specific review method.
Export selected material to Anki or printable cards when you want additional review outside a quiz.

See Which review should I use? for a concise comparison of the available review options.

Browser and application lifecycle

DLMS runs as a local application/server process, and the browser is its interface. In normal local desktop mode, Shutdown DLMS is the immediate and recommended way to stop the application.

In the normal loopback desktop configuration, closing the final DLMS browser window may trigger automatic shutdown after a grace period when browser-presence shutdown is enabled.
If local desktop DLMS is still running, reopen your browser and visit http://127.0.0.1:9001/ instead of starting another server copy.
In explicit LAN/server mode, in-app browser/API shutdown and automatic browser-presence shutdown are unavailable. Closing client browser windows does not stop the server. Stop the DLMS process or service from the host computer—for example, from the terminal, service manager, or process manager that started it.
Windows, macOS, and Linux: Closing a browser window is not an unconditional shutdown command. Shutdown availability follows the configured runtime mode, not the address of the browser making a request.

Install DLMS on macOS

The packaged macOS release is an Apple Silicon ZIP containing a native DLMS.app bundle.

Download and extract DLMS-3.2.2-macos-arm64.zip (latest published release; 3.3.0 packages are pending).
Drag DLMS.app into /Applications.
Open DLMS from Finder or Applications. DLMS starts locally and normally opens its browser interface.
If Gatekeeper blocks the app, which is not Developer ID-signed or notarized, where available Control-click DLMS.app, choose Open, and confirm. Otherwise open System Settings → Privacy & Security, choose Open Anyway, and confirm.
Architecture: The documented macOS release is for Apple Silicon (arm64), not Intel. Quarantine removal with xattr is a troubleshooting fallback, not a normal installation step.

Local and trusted-network use

DLMS always defaults to 127.0.0.1:9001. Headless or SSH detection controls automatic browser launch only; it does not change this network bind.

For intentional trusted-LAN or server access, launch explicitly with --host 0.0.0.0.
Use the server's actual LAN address, such as http://192.168.1.25:9001, from another device. 0.0.0.0 is a bind address, not a browser destination.
Intentional separation: Do not change or rely on headless detection to make DLMS LAN-accessible. Binding to 0.0.0.0 exposes DLMS to devices that can reach the host and port. DLMS has no login-based access control, so use this only on a trusted LAN with suitable firewall restrictions and never expose it directly to the public internet.
