External AI Quiz Builder

Use a conversational AI to produce structured text, then let DLMS validate, review, and publish it as a normal quiz.

What it does

External AI Quiz Builder generates a provider-neutral prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, a local AI, or another conversational AI. No API key is required. DLMS does not connect to an AI, send the prompt, or retrieve a response automatically; you copy and paste the text manually.

Choose Choice questions for single- or multiple-answer questions, or Matching / terminology for one term-and-definition matching activity. Both paths produce structured JSON text and use the same validation, temporary staging, Review & Repair, and normal quiz publication safeguards.

Prompt, paste, review, publish

Choose the content workflow. Configure the topic, audience, difficulty, source expectations, and either question count or matching-pair count.
Generate and copy the prompt. Use it with the conversational AI of your choice. DLMS does not send anything to that provider.
Paste and validate the response. Copy the structured JSON response into DLMS. It is treated as untrusted text and checked before use.
Review and publish. AI answers may be wrong, and AI pairings may be wrong. Repair every kept item and explicitly confirm the complete answer set or every matching pair before publication.

Choice-question responses

Choice questions support single- and multiple-answer modes. Each question must have 2–26 choices, all valid, and DLMS assigns the visible A–Z labels. Review & Repair requires confirmation of the complete correct-answer set; wording such as “choose two” may establish answer mode but does not prove which choices are correct.

Matching and terminology responses

The matching workflow accepts one matching question containing 2–100 complete, distinct term/definition pairs. You can choose term → definition, definition → term, or a random direction, and set how many pairs appear in an attempt.

Review & Repair lets you edit, add, remove, and reorder pairs; correct OCR- or AI-like wording errors; set optional categories and explanations; and confirm every final pairing. Missing sides, duplicate or conflicting pair mappings, malformed fields, unsupported structures, and out-of-range counts remain blocking until repaired or excluded.

The text workflows and ZIP workflow are deliberately separate. None requires DLMS to call an AI-provider API.

Privacy and temporary data

The raw AI response is retained only in the temporary review draft. Start Over or successful publication removes the draft; abandoned drafts expire automatically. Raw response text is not stored in the published quiz.
