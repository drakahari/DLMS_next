External AI Quiz Builder

Use a conversational AI to produce structured text, then let DLMS validate, review, and publish it as a normal quiz.

Help topicsGetting StartedTaking QuizzesBuilding QuizzesExternal AI Quiz BuilderPDF & Image ImportStudy PacksTroubleshooting

What it does

External AI Quiz Builder generates a provider-neutral prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, a local AI, or another conversational AI. No API key is required. DLMS does not connect to an AI, send the prompt, or retrieve a response automatically; you copy and paste the text manually.

Workflow

Generate and copy the prompt. Configure the topic, audience, difficulty, question count, and source expectations.
Paste and validate the response. Copy the AI's structured JSON response into DLMS. It is treated as untrusted text and validated before use.
Review and publish. AI answers may be wrong. Repair every kept question and explicitly confirm the complete correct-answer set before publishing.

Single- and multiple-answer questions are supported. Each question may have 2–26 choices, with DLMS assigning A–Z labels.

How it differs from AI Study Pack Builder

WorkflowAI returnsDLMS creates
External AI Quiz BuilderStructured JSON text that you paste into DLMSA normal quiz after validation and Review & Repair

Neither workflow replaces the other.

Privacy and temporary data

The raw AI response is retained only in the temporary review draft. Start Over or successful publication removes the draft; abandoned drafts expire automatically. Raw response text is not stored in the published quiz.
