Settings & Personalization

Change presentation and workflow preferences without changing your study data.

Appearance and themes

Open Settings → Appearance to choose Dark, Light, Purple & Gold, or Maroon & Gold, change the dashboard title, or set a background image. Themes change presentation only; they do not change grading, content, history, or Learning Intelligence.

Customize Navigation

Open Settings → Navigation to show or hide IT Study, Law Study, Medical Study, and Other Studies in the sidebar. All four are visible by default.

Navigation only: hiding an area does not delete, disable, or alter its quizzes, Study Packs, cases, history, analytics, or direct URLs. Study Packs and Settings always remain visible. Re-enable an area at any time to restore its link.

AI and parsing

AI Integration configures optional providers and editable prompt templates for supported workflows. DLMS works without an AI provider. Parsing changes how future copied/uploaded text is interpreted; it does not rewrite existing quizzes.

Backup, reset, and removal

Use Backup & Restore to create a portable backup or validate and restore an existing backup. Use Reset & Remove to clear persistent exam results, run a scoped reset, or remove DLMS data after reviewing what each action preserves and removes.

Before a major change: download a backup and keep it outside the DLMS data directory if you need an independent recovery copy.

System Tools

System Tools contains advanced operational actions such as rebuilding generated quiz pages and opening Image Study Editor. It is not a required part of ordinary study or quiz creation.
