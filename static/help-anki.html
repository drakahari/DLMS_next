Anki Tools

Anki Tools turns DLMS study material into spaced-repetition exports. The main page includes Custom Deck and Law Study Anki sub-workflows, with export routes for quiz material, missed questions, study content, and law review material.

Export missed questions from review

DLMS can turn questions you missed into an Anki deck instead of forcing you to re-export an entire quiz. In review, select individual questions with Add to Anki deck, or use the option to select all missed questions, then export the resulting APKG deck.

This workflow is useful for targeted remediation: DLMS identifies what you missed, while Anki handles long-term spaced repetition of that smaller set.

Review the exported material in Anki

After importing the DLMS export, the cards become part of the normal Anki study workflow. Review the imported deck in Anki and use Anki's scheduling controls as you would with any other deck.

Law Study Anki

The Law Study Anki workflow is tailored to case-review material and is separate from the general custom-deck workflow.

Recommended use

Finish or review material in DLMS first so you know what is worth retaining.
Choose the export source: a quiz, missed questions, custom cards, or law-study material.
Import the resulting Anki-compatible export into your Anki collection and confirm field mapping before a large import.

Printable flashcards

The Custom Deck workspace can send the same selected quiz, missed-question, and Law Study cards to an Avery 5388 print layout. Template 5388 uses 3 × 5 inch index cards with three cards per US Letter sheet. DLMS creates alternating front/back sheets for duplex printing and offers long-edge or short-edge back ordering. Print one test sheet before a large batch.
