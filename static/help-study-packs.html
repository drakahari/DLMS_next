Installed Study Packs

Study Packs are reusable content packages that can contain matching datasets, image/hotspot datasets, and mixed quiz datasets. The Study Packs page is the learner-facing library: expand a pack to see the activities it provides and generate focused practice.

AI Study Pack Builder

The AI Study Pack Builder is a prompt-construction tool for creating source-disciplined, DLMS-ready Study Pack content with an AI provider. It does not silently generate or install a pack by itself. Instead, it builds a controlled prompt that tells the selected AI exactly what DLMS expects.

What you control

SettingWhat it changes
What do you want to study?The exact subject or topic the AI should build study content around.
Subject / DomainApplies domain-aware instructions for IT/Cybersecurity, Medical, Science, History, Language, General, or other material.
DifficultyRequests Foundational, Intermediate, or Comprehensive depth.
Pack SizeControls the requested breadth of the resulting datasets without encouraging filler.
Images Requested / Image StyleControls whether the prompt requests legally reusable images or diagrams and the preferred visual style.
AI ProviderSelects ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, or a configured local/custom provider for the Copy Prompt & Open AI action.
Matching / TerminologyRequests matching datasets with concise answers and source-supported Study Mode explanations.
Images / DiagramsRequests image/hotspot datasets when the subject benefits from visual identification.

What DLMS adds to the prompt

  • Source and accuracy requirements that tell the AI to research before creating content and to omit unverifiable facts instead of guessing.
  • DLMS dataset/schema expectations so the returned material can be packaged as a Study Pack.
  • Licensing and provenance requirements for reusable images.
  • Instructions to keep concise matching answers separate from richer Study Mode explanations.
  • Domain-specific safeguards. Choosing Medical, for example, adds stricter medical sourcing, licensing, provenance, and non-synthetic-image requirements.
Recommended workflow: configure the request → Generate AI Prompt → review/edit the generated prompt if needed → Copy Prompt or Copy Prompt & Open AI → have the AI create the Study Pack files → install the finished ZIP through Content Packs, where DLMS independently validates it before installation.
The AI provider's output is not trusted automatically. A generated Study Pack still has to satisfy DLMS Content Pack validation. Source claims, licenses, files, dataset structure, IDs, image references, and other pack requirements are checked independently during import.

Study Packs vs. Content Packs

Study Packs is where users launch installed study material. Content Packs is the management layer used to validate, inspect, import, export, and delete the installed pack folders that supply that study content.

The Manage Packs action from Study Packs leads into Content Pack management. After management, returning to Study Packs shows the learner-facing result.
