Study Packs & AI Builder

Create reusable study material, validate it safely, then choose what to study.

Use an installed Study Pack

Open Study Packs to browse installed content. Expand a pack, choose the dataset or activity you want, and generate focused practice. A pack can contain matching, single-select multiple-choice, mixed, or image/hotspot material.

Create a Study Pack with an external AI

Configure. Open Study Packs → Create Study Pack and choose the topic, domain, difficulty, size, and requested content types.
Generate Prompt. Copy the DLMS prompt or open your selected AI provider.
Bring Back ZIP. Ask the AI to return a valid DLMS Study Pack ZIP, then upload that ZIP in the Builder. Plain pasted AI prose is not installable.
Validate. DLMS shows errors and warnings. Correct blocking errors in the pack and upload again. Review warning-only packs and explicitly confirm before installation.
Install. DLMS uses the same trusted Content Pack validation and installation process as manual imports.
Study. After installation, DLMS returns to Study Packs and identifies the installed pack. Choose the activity you want; DLMS does not automatically generate a quiz.

Request multiple-choice questions

Select Multiple-Choice Questions in the Builder when they suit the topic. AI-generated Study Pack MCQs are single-select: one choice is marked correct and DLMS assigns the visible A–Z labels.

Accuracy first: ask for questions supported by reliable source material. The AI should not guess a correct answer, invent a citation, or force a question when the evidence is uncertain. It should omit unsupported questions, use plausible but incorrect distractors, and give a concise explanation that agrees with the marked answer.

Use small, reusable question-level concepts when questions assess the same skill. Concepts help Learning Intelligence connect Study and Exam evidence to a topic; tags remains accepted for compatible older packs.

Manual or external packs

Content Packs remains the place to import, inspect, export, and remove packs obtained outside the guided Builder. The validation rules are the same: malformed, unsafe, or incomplete packs are not installed.

Images, provenance, matching, mixed questions, hotspots, and concepts are preserved through validation and installation when the pack is valid.
