Learning Intelligence & Review Planning

Use tagged question evidence to decide what to review next.

Concepts connect questions to topics

Questions can carry small, reusable concepts, such as octal-permissions or file-ownership. Older compatible tags are accepted, but concepts are the current field. Use the same spelling when questions assess the same skill.

Untagged questions still work, score normally, and remain in History. They simply cannot contribute evidence to a concept-level topic, so they may not appear in mastery or weak-area views.

Choose your Learning Scope

Learning Scope answers “What am I actively studying now?” All folders start included. Open Manage Learning Scope from Topic Intelligence, Today’s Review, or Quiz Library to include or exclude a folder, including Uncategorized. The folder list also shows empty and hidden folders.

Excluded quizzes remain saved and playable. Their history, scores, concepts, and answer evidence remain intact, while current Learning Intelligence, Learning Profile, Diagnostics, Due Questions, Topic Retention, Adaptive Study, Smart Review, Concept Review, Today’s Review recommendations, and the Library Needs Review view leave them out. History, Analytics, missed-question review, and manual Mixed Quiz selection still cover the full Library. Hiding a folder only changes Library visibility; it does not change Learning Scope.

Generated practice follows each original source question’s folder. If you answer an excluded quiz manually, DLMS still saves the result. Schedules keep advancing with time; including the folder again uses the saved evidence, so questions may be due or overdue immediately. Unfinished Resume cards saved in this browser stay available.

How evidence follows a question

Study Mode records answer interactions. Exam Mode records completed attempt responses. Learning Intelligence combines this evidence across quizzes and Study Packs through each question’s saved concepts.

New generated practice keeps a durable link to its original source question. Results from Smart, Concept, Adaptive, Due Questions, and other generated sessions therefore strengthen the source question’s history instead of becoming unrelated evidence. Independently authored questions with identical wording remain separate; older quizzes without explicit lineage use a conservative compatibility fallback rather than guessed relationships.

If Study Mode cannot save an interaction, it shows Learning progress was not saved. You can keep studying and use Retry without resetting the current question.

Use enough evidence: one answer is a hint, not a conclusion. DLMS de-duplicates repeated interactions and waits for enough evidence before classifying a topic as weak or strong.

Understand concept performance

Open Learning Intelligence to see how many source questions and quizzes cover each concept, how many answers are recorded, correct and incorrect results, overall accuracy, recent accuracy, trend, and mastery.

Recent accuracy uses up to the five latest deduplicated answers. A trend appears only after at least six answers, when DLMS can compare two recent evidence windows. Smaller changes are shown as roughly stable; improving or declining requires a meaningful difference. Fewer than three answers remains Not enough data rather than receiving a weak or strong label.

Mastery is an explainable study signal, not a course grade or diagnosis. A low or missing value often means you need more tagged practice, not that DLMS has judged the topic permanently. Use How mastery works in the workspace for the current calculation.

Due Questions and Topic Retention Schedule

Due Questions is the individual question schedule. Independent first success in a new Study review advances through simple intervals only when the previous interval has elapsed. A first mistake or prior feedback/tool action keeps a short interval. Legacy Study and Exam results keep their existing scheduling interpretation. Unseen questions are listed as not yet scheduled until their first recorded answer.

Today’s Review shows the total number currently due. Its next Due Questions session includes up to 20 questions, or all due questions when fewer than 20 remain. For a different batch size, Review Schedule offers 10, 20, 30, or 50 questions and shows how that choice compares with the current due total. After a completed batch is saved, DLMS recalculates the remaining due count; start another batch if questions are still due.

The Topic Retention Schedule is concept-level. It estimates when a tagged topic needs reinforcement using mastery and elapsed time. Base mastery and retained mastery remain separate, so an overdue estimate does not rewrite recorded answers or accuracy.

Diagnostics and confusion patterns

Learning Diagnostics can surface repeated choice confusions and question-quality review signals. Treat these as prompts to inspect a topic or question, not automatic proof that content is wrong. Choice-confusion information applies only where answer choices make sense; matching and hotspot responses are not forced into that model.

What to do next

Start on the Dashboard with Today’s Review when you want DLMS to choose the most useful current action.
If you want a broad personalized mix, choose Study What I Need Most. If you want one known topic, choose its Study concept action.
Use Due Questions for individual scheduled questions and Topic Retention Schedule for concept-level timing.
Use History’s missed-question review for one completed attempt. If results look surprising across attempts, inspect Learning Diagnostics.

New Study evidence and historical coverage

New Study sessions preserve first graded outcomes and observable feedback/actions separately from corrections. Learning estimates use independent first success; a later correction does not erase initial difficulty. Review completion is coverage, not a passing prediction. Legacy evidence retains its older interpretation.

Study History survives a Learning Intelligence reset. Earlier facts do not silently rebuild reset estimates. Read the Study save, resume and completion guide.

Exam Plans: choose what to study next

An optional exam plan turns your selected material and planned study time into a daily question target. It estimates work; it does not measure active study minutes or predict whether you will pass.

Create a plan · Study today · Understand progress · No suggested work? · Time estimates · Change or recover a plan

Create a plan

  1. Open Learning Intelligence → Exam Plans → New exam plan, or create a plan here.
  2. Enter your certification name and exam date. Check the Plan calendar timezone. Your saved dates and study days stay in that timezone when you travel.
  3. Under Study material, choose the exact folders you want. Folders are flat: there are no subfolders. Learning Scope exclusions still apply. A hidden Library folder is a separate choice.
  4. Choose your Study days and Minutes you plan to study on each selected day. Adjust Pace and review assumptions if the starting estimates do not suit you.
  5. Calculate the preview. Select Use this plan on my dashboard if wanted, then Save plan.

You can save several plans, but only one appears on the dashboard. Selecting another names the plan it will replace; it does not delete it. Leaving the checkbox unchecked preserves the current selection. Ordinary edits do not switch plans.

Start today’s practice

On the dashboard, look for Your exam plan in Today’s Review. Check its certification name, date and status. When work is ready, Start suggested work opens focused practice. The question count and estimated time describe that practice set. View plan opens controls and other practice choices.

The short breakdown shows questions with No recorded answer, questions needing Coverage / fresh evidence, Mistakes, Due reviews and any Other practice. A question appears in only one count even when several reasons apply. “No recorded answer” does not prove you have never seen it. Why this? explains the selection.

DLMS spreads first-pass work over your remaining study days, within the daily budget you chose. It uses remaining room for mistakes and review. Work saved today reduces today’s allowance; refreshing does not assign a full new quota. Optional extra practice does not use future days’ allowance. Your last regular quiz and any unfinished Resume option stay separate from generated practice.

Wrong answers and progress

A complete wrong answer counts as reviewed. Correcting it later keeps the first mistake visible to Learning Intelligence. An assisted answer may count toward finishing, but it is distinct from independent success. Use Finish Review and wait for confirmation; answering the last question alone does not finish the review. A focused quiz finishes only its selected set, not a whole source quiz.

Included material reviewed compares reviewed questions with all included questions, including currently unavailable ones. Excluded questions are shown separately and are not counted as finished. Expand Study evidence and history for partial sessions, full reviews, focused sessions, corrections and assistance. Reviewed does not mean mastered.

A Learning Intelligence reset keeps factual Study history but clears learning estimates. DLMS may then ask for fresh answers; retained history does not rebuild the cleared mistake counts or review schedule.

Why Start suggested work may be unavailable

  • Excluded or missing material: follow the Learning Scope or Edit plan link. Choosing a folder never removes exclusions automatically. Restore unavailable files or choose other included material.
  • Suggestions paused: resume suggestions on the plan page when ready. Showing a paused plan does not unpause it.
  • Not a study day or today’s allowance used: check the next selected study date. Choose optional practice if you want to do more.
  • Only future reviews remain: check the next scheduled review date. Future reviews contribute to the workload estimate but are not all due today.
  • Exam today, in the past, or no study days left: no study capacity remains before that exam date. Edit the date only if your actual plans change.

The review-due date follows spaced review; your next study day follows the saved plan calendar. They may differ. DLMS does not move the exam or shorten review intervals to make the work fit.

Understand time estimates: one example

Suppose you have six study days left, at 30 minutes per day: 180 minutes available. Eighty questions at two minutes each, plus a 25% review allowance, need about 200 minutes. The plan warns of a shortfall. It needs about 14 questions per day for coverage, but your budget fits about 12. If you answer five of those today, about seven slots remain—not another full set of twelve.

You can have ready practice and still have too much overall work for your budget. Study time available ends the day before the exam. Estimated work remaining includes current coverage gaps, mistake follow-up and reviews scheduled before the exam. Expand Estimate assumptions and uncertainty for exact values and the low/high range. Harder questions, new mistakes and added material may take more time; a positive balance does not prove exam readiness.

Change, hide or recover a plan

Hide from dashboard changes visibility. Pause suggestions stops recommendations. Neither deletes history. If Today’s Review is hidden, enable it in Layout & navigation; choosing a plan does not override that setting. Delete removes only the plan.

Folders update as quizzes are added, moved or deleted. Changes to study material groups changes by quiz, with expandable question pages. Mark these changes as seen acknowledges that displayed summary, not study progress. If material changes again before saving, reload and check the new summary. Renamed folders need explicit selection in Edit plan. Changed questions may require fresh evidence; DLMS does not substitute old answers for revised material.

A failed save keeps your previous plan. If another tab changed it, reload before editing again. If creating practice loses its confirmation, use Retry same request to recover the same quiz. If DLMS was interrupted, allow its next normal startup to recover, then check Generated Practice before explicitly starting replacement work. Do not clear browser storage to force a second quiz.

Backups include plans and durable marks. Backups made before a feature was added may lack its records; existing saved plans and Study history are preserved. After restoring, reload old tabs. Saved activity times display in your browser’s local timezone; the plan’s calendar dates keep their chosen timezone.
